Common questions

How do I move my data to a new iPhone?

Backups are a Pro feature. Open Backups, tap the three dots, choose Save now, and send the file to the new phone (AirDrop or the Files app). On the new phone open Backups, choose Import From File and pick it. Choose Combine to add it to existing data, or Remove existing data to replace everything.

I can't unlock the app.

The app lock (a Pro setting) uses Face ID or your iPhone passcode, not a password of its own. If Face ID does not work, use your iPhone passcode when it asks. If you have forgotten your iPhone passcode, you need Apple's help to reset it (support.apple.com/iphone-passcode).

How do I cancel my subscription?

On your iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Debtuo → Cancel. Deleting the app does not cancel it.

I bought Pro but it is not active.

Open Pro Mode (tap PRO on the main screen), choose Turn on, then "Restore purchases". Make sure you are signed in with the Apple ID you bought it with.

Where are my backup files?

In the Files app: On My iPhone → Debtuo.
