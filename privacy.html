Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026

Debtuo is built so that your information stays with you. This page says exactly what the app does with it.

Your data stays on your device

The contacts, amounts, comments and history you enter are stored only on your iPhone. Debtuo has no accounts and no servers of its own, so we never receive this data and cannot read it.

Contacts access (optional)

If you allow it, Debtuo reads the names in your phone book to suggest a name while you type. The names are used on the device only and are never uploaded or stored by the app. You can turn this off at any time in iPhone Settings. Everything works without it.

App lock and Face ID

If you turn on the app lock (a Pro setting), Debtuo asks iOS to check you with Face ID, Touch ID or your iPhone passcode. The check is done by iOS; Debtuo never sees your biometric data or your passcode, and keeps no password of its own.

Backups

Backups are files created by you or by the app's automatic daily backup, saved in the app's folder on your iPhone, which you can see in the Files app. If you share a backup file or turn on iCloud backup (a Pro feature), the file goes where you send it, or to your own iCloud Drive, under Apple's terms. Backups are encrypted with a key built into the app by default; this hides them from casual reading but is not strong protection against someone who has both the file and the app.

Purchases

Pro is sold through the App Store. Apple processes the payment; we never see your payment details. To check what you have bought, the app uses RevenueCat, a subscription service. RevenueCat receives your App Store purchase receipt and an anonymous identifier generated by the app (not linked to your name, email or Apple ID) so it can tell the app whether Pro is active. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

What we do not do

  • No advertising and no ad networks.
  • No tracking across other companies' apps and websites.
  • No analytics that identify you. The "Anonymous statistics" switch in Settings is off by default and, in this version, sends nothing.
  • We do not sell or share your data.

Children

Debtuo is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect any personal information.

Deleting your data

Delete the app to remove its data from your iPhone. Backup files you saved in the Files app or in iCloud Drive stay until you delete them there.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
