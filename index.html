Debtuo: who owes whom

Debtuo is a simple iPhone app for keeping track of money you lent and money you borrowed. Add a person, write down amounts, see every balance at a glance and keep a history you can edit. Your data stays on your phone.

Free, always: unlimited contacts and history, calculator, search, dark mode, English and Russian. No ads.

Pro (optional): encrypted backups (Files app, iCloud Drive, daily automatic, import from a file), CSV import and export for Excel, and an app lock with Face ID or your iPhone passcode. A monthly or yearly subscription, or a one-time lifetime purchase.
